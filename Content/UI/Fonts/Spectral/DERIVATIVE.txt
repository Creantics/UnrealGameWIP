SpectralSCBaked-Medium.ttf and SpectralSCBaked-SemiBold.ttf are Modified Versions of the Spectral SC fonts
(Copyright 2017 The Spectral Project Authors), released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt in this folder).

What changed: the small-caps forms are baked into the default glyphs, so lowercase input draws as small caps
without OpenType features.

Names: the Spectral fonts declare no Reserved Font Name. Even so, the modified files do not use the original
family name inside the font. Their name tables say "FT Baked Serif SC" (family) and "FTBakedSerifSC-Medium" /
"FTBakedSerifSC-SemiBold" (PostScript name). The unmodified Spectral-Medium.ttf and Spectral-SemiBold.ttf files keep
their original names.
